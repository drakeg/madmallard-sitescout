What SiteScout actually does

SiteScout is an early-stage website inspection and improvement planner, not a full website audit or a redesign generator yet. It captures the source HTML of up to three authorized HTTPS pages, examines what's declared in that HTML, and helps you decide what deserves manual testing or improvement.

1. Collect pages

Start with one authorized public HTTPS URL. It follows a small number of same-host links, prioritizing contact and booking pages.

2. Inspect evidence

Record page titles, descriptions, headings, language, mobile viewport declarations, images, links and form fields/actions.

3. Prioritize work

Flag observable issues, explain why they matter, show the page and evidence, and propose a fix plus a verification step.

4. Export findings

Download a printable customer review, an action-plan CSV, and form testing checklist. Human testing and sign-off are still required.

Not tested: rendered layout, mobile usability, Lighthouse performance, WCAG conformance, SEO rankings, broken links, JavaScript, customer journey completion, form delivery, payments, bookings, or CRM integrations. Uncaptured pages are unknown, not broken. Findings are review suggestions, not certifications.

Analyze a website URL

Enter an authorized public HTTPS page URL. SiteScout securely retrieves the requested page and up to two same-host linked pages on an enabled, trusted Docker host. You do not need to paste HTML.

Up to three same-host HTTPS pages per request; limited HTML link discovery, no JavaScript execution, forms, or outreach. Docker operators can enable restricted trusted-LAN mode without a token prompt. Other installations require a private token; use localhost or an HTTPS reverse proxy before entering credentials.
